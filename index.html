Ruletape

The beats roulette: drop the needle and discover random YouTube instrumentals filtered by genre, era, length and views. A turntable arm drops on the record like a vinyl player, the beat loops, and favorites sync across devices with Google sign-in. Smart caching (pools in edge storage) keeps the daily YouTube API quota near zero, so the roulette never runs out of beats.

Technologies: JavaScript, Cloudflare Workers (serverless), KV storage, YouTube Data API, OAuth 2.0 Google + PKCE.

Notes_VTT

Progressive web app for writing song notes and lyrics, with chips linking to YouTube beats. Google sign-in, favorites and real-time sync across devices — the same beat you were writing over follows you to your phone. Installable and usable offline.

Technologies: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Firebase (Firestore + Auth).
